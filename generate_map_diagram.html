03 Multi-Head
Multi-Head Subspaces, Causal Masks & KV-Cache
Splits the stream into h parallel heads (syntax, facts, logic). Applies lower-triangular causal masks for generation and caches past keys/values for O(1) inference.

The persistent conveyor belt of the network. Additive skip connections prevent vanishing gradients across 100+ layers. RMSNorm stabilizes signal variance.
